BLIND FLIGHT

One flies. One sees. Together you make it through the course.

How to play
  • The pilot flies the plane from the cockpit but can only see a short way ahead – the fog is thick.
  • The navigator sees a radar with every obstacle and gate and tells the pilot how to fly: "turn right", "climb", "brake"...
  • Fly through the gates in order. Crash = +3 s, missed gate = +5 s, star = −1 s.
  • Talk to each other with the in-game voice chat, or call each other.
  • Pilot controls: W/S or ↑/↓ = up/down, A/D or ←/→ = bank and turn, Space = throttle, Shift = brake (good before sharp turns), Q/E = look around. On mobile: stick on the left, buttons on the right, drag on the right side of the screen to look around.
  • The navigator can point with the arrow buttons ← ↑ ↓ →. Your friend in the cockpit then points too. The route you draw shows up as faint dots for the pilot.
